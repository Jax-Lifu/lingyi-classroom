Change notice, 2026-11-20: Selected 300 characters, retained the original strokes and medians fields and combined the entries in play-strokes.json. The SVG path strings, median coordinates, point order and stroke order are unmodified. This selected dataset is distributed under the Arphic Public License.

原有 100 字的首笔与笔数核查：逐字对照教育部、国家语言文字工作委员会 GF 0023—2020《通用规范汉字笔顺规范》；首笔按横、竖、撇、点、折五类核对，笔数按该字的规范表行核对。独立笔画练习的中线指引仍使用上游给出的手写轨迹。
核查参考：https://jwc.hntou.edu.cn/yywz/zlxz/202403/P020240321636511685938.pdf
